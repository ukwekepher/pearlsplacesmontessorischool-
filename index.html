BRIEF HISTORY OF PEARL'S PLACE MONTESSORI SCHOOL

A bright beginning.
A brilliant future.

Pearl's Place Montessori School was established in September 2021 by the grace of God, out of a vision, passion, and commitment to make a meaningful and lasting impact on the younger generation through quality education.

Read more Read less

The school was founded by Mrs May Ndubuisi Ezekiel and Mr Ndubuisi Ezekiel, both of whom have extensive experience in the education sector. Over the years, they have served as teachers and held various leadership positions in several reputable educational institutions, acquiring valuable experience and expertise in the field of education.

Mrs May Ndubuisi Ezekiel is a certified Montessori educator and a registered member of the Teachers Registration Council of Nigeria (TRCN), among other professional qualifications. Her passion for child-centred learning and holistic development continues to contribute significantly to the school's educational philosophy and standards.

Pearl's Place Montessori School is duly registered with the Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC) of Nigeria. Its registration, administrative and operational documentation are supported by a team of professionals from diverse fields, including Education, Medicine, Engineering and other relevant disciplines.

In its unwavering commitment to quality education, professional excellence and regulatory compliance, the school operates in line with relevant Federal Government educational policies and approved curriculum guidelines. It is also a proud member of the National Association of Proprietors of Private Schools (NAPPS), reflecting its dedication to upholding professional standards, embracing best practices and promoting continuous improvement in the education sector.

At Pearl's Place Montessori, we believe that every child is unique and possesses enormous potential waiting to be discovered and nurtured. Consequently, the school is equipped with qualified, competent and dedicated educators who are committed to providing a safe, healthy, stimulating and conducive learning environment. We strive to nurture the whole child by promoting academic excellence, social competence, moral values, creativity, independence and intellectual development.

Since its establishment, the school has continued to experience steady growth and remarkable progress. Through the dedication of its management and staff, Pearl's Place Montessori has continued to make a positive impact on the academic growth, character formation and overall development of its pupils, all by the grace of God.

At Pearl's Place Montessori, we are committed to laying a solid foundation for lifelong learning and raising confident, responsible, independent, and well-rounded children who are equipped to thrive in an ever-changing world.

We look forward to welcoming your child into the Pearl's Place Montessori family, where every child is valued, every potential is nurtured, and every learning experience matters.
